    About the project, reuse and citation

    Each edition pairs the original digitised pages with a searchable transcription. Depending on the work, it may also include transliteration, an English digest and an entity index.

    Licence. Transcriptions, transliterations, digests and indexes are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal. Facsimile images remain subject to the terms of their holding institutions; check the library record before reusing an image.
